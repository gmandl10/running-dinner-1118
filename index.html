Running Dinner

What is a Running Dinner?

A Running Dinner is a progressive social meal where each participant is responsible for hosting one meal course (either Starter or Main) at their home for 2 guests, while attending the other course as a guest at someone else's place. At 20:30, all 30 guests come together at one venue for dessert and the after-party!
