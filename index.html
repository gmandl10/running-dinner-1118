Running Dinner

What is a Running Dinner?

A Running Dinner is a social meal where each participant is responsible for hosting one course (either Appetizer, Main, or Dessert) at their home for 2 guests, while attending the other course as a guest at someone else's place. At 20:30, all guests come together for dessert and the after-party!
